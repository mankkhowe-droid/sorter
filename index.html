Matched by substrings in each domain's live records, checked in this order: MX, then NS, then self-hosting, then SPF (finds the real provider behind Mimecast/Proofpoint-style filters). Within a pass, the first provider in this list wins. Edits are saved in this browser. Wrap a pattern in slashes for regex, e.g. /^mail\./. Office 365, Google Workspace, Yahoo and GoDaddy have strong, reliable DNS signatures. cPanel-powered / Roundcube webmail can't be fingerprinted from DNS alone (they're software running on top of any mail server) — that bucket instead catches domains whose mail routes back to their own domain, i.e. self-hosted mail, which covers most cPanel/Roundcube setups and generic ZA webmail resellers.
DNS resolved via Google & Cloudflare public DoH resolvers, client-side. Nothing is sent to a server other than the DNS lookups themselves.
